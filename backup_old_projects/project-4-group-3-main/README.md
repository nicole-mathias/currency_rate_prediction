#### Files:
* `dt_importance.py` : Decision tree regressor used to find importance features for both countries (US & Japan). Note that the code contains the important attributes only, rest all were dropped in the code based on previous analysis and iterations of mean square error and importance score.
  
* `volatility.py` : Used to answer a data science question by looking at all the past economic factors (US & Japan) and plotting a plotly graph. Question: By looking at the historical data how volatile is the exchange rate?.
  
* `candlestick.py` : Used for two purposes (US stock market - S&P_500):
  * (1) To plot (plotly) stock market "daily candlesticks" over the past 40 years
  * (2) To plot (plotly) a close up view of the candlesticks over a short period of time, so that the candlesticks are clearly visible in order to provide some knowledge about how to read and interpret these Japanese candlesticks.
    
* `candle_average.py` : (US stock market - S&P_500)
  * Since we have plenty of daily data over the past 40 years, I have plotted the monthly average's over the past years. This was done to just check the overall view of how the market average has looked over the past years (because it is diffuclt to interpret 40 years worth of daily data)
